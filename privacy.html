Looply Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

Looply is designed with privacy in mind.

Looply does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

All events, history, predictions, and reminders are stored locally on your device.

Looply does not require an account.

Looply does not use analytics services.

Looply does not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Local notifications are generated on your device. No notification data is sent to any server.

If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated before any data collection is introduced.
