FAQ

What is Looply?

Looply helps you remember recurring things by learning from what you log.

Does Looply store data in the cloud?

No. Looply currently stores data locally on your device.

Do I need an account?

No. Looply does not require an account.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete events in the app. More export and reset options are planned.

How do reminders work?

Looply schedules local reminders based on predicted next dates.
